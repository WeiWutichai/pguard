PDProduct decision (recommended)

Should guard have any access to payment / wallet / refund flows?

Option A — Guard stays out of money (recommended)

  • Guard sees own earnings (read-only) and tip notifications
  • No access to /admin/wallet, /admin/payments, /admin/refunds, /pricing CRUD
  • No write on anything monetary except progress reports (feeds proration)
  • Refund handled by admin; tip handled by customer

Why: minimum necessary access (PDPA principle), reduces blast radius if credentials leak, admin-mediated dispute resolution is cleaner.

Option B — Guard as cashier (not recommended now)

  • Guard can record cash collection on behalf of platform
  • Guard can issue receipts on customer's behalf
  • Guard has limited write on payment ledger

Requires: separate cashier sub-role, separate audit trail, separate UI in mobile, separate testing. Not in current scope.

Current code follows Option A. Recommendation: keep Option A. Re-evaluate when business model needs cash collection (currently digital payments only).
